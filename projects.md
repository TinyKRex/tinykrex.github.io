# Projects

## MetaForge — Deep Learning-Based Discovery of Moonlighting Enzymes
*IDEA-B Synthetic Biology Competition, MSIT / Korea Synthetic Biology Council · Mar. – Oct. 2026*
*Team of 6 · my role: candidate selection and validation design*

Our team passed the first round of review and the project was funded as a commissioned research
project at KRIBB.

The first job was justifying the premise. A literature and database review established that
multifunctional proteins are heavily over-represented among proteins linked to drug side
effects — which made the single-function assumption behind conventional enzyme discovery the
gap worth attacking.

The second was making the work fit an eight-month budget. From BRENDA, UniProt and prior
literature we collected 14 enzyme–substrate reactions, then ran pre-screening controls to drop
combinations where the biosensor cross-detected the substrate itself or lacked sensitivity. That
cut half the initial candidates, but the surviving **7 reaction systems** let us run
**380 metagenomic candidates × 7 substrates × 4 reaction conditions — roughly 10,000 conditions
in three days**.

## MAKEAT — AI Diet-Tracking App
*K-Software Empowerment BootCamp (640 h), Sungkyunkwan University · 2022 – 2023*
*Excellence Award, healthcare track*

A mobile app that logs meals automatically by classifying photographs of food. I handled
database management and volunteered for the Android/Flutter development role when no one else
wanted it, learning the stack from scratch during the project.

Individual projects in the same program: an oral medication image classifier, and an analysis of
COVID-19 case data.
