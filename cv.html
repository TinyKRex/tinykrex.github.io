Research Experience

Graduate Researcher — Synthetic Biology and Bioengineering Research Center, KRIBB

Feb 2025 – present, Daejeon · KAIST–KRIBB joint graduate program

Building an iterative Design–Build–Test–Learn workflow for enzyme discovery from metagenomes, in which protein language model embeddings select candidates and cell-free protein synthesis on a biofoundry measures what they do.

  • Generated ProtT5 embeddings for ~5.3M ORFs from 16 environmental metagenomes as a 1,024-dimensional vector database, and selected candidates by cosine similarity to the mean embedding of 98 structurally characterized bacterial lipases, recovering sequences too distant for homology search to return.
  • Standardized the validation workflow so that rounds are comparable: tailing PCR with T7 promoter/terminator in the primer 5′ tails shortened library construction; in-house S30 extract from a GroEL/GroES-overexpressing strain removed batch-to-batch variation, gave ~2.5× the expression yield of a commercial kit by GFP comparison, and detected pNPO/pNPB/pNPP activity in the CalB control that the kit had not.
  • Screened 855 candidate ORFs from 9 libraries against 3 pNP substrates on a 96/384-well automated workflow (Echo 525 acoustic dispenser, automated liquid handlers). A robust Z-score criterion (Z ≥ 3 plus positive-control comparison) replaced ad-hoc thresholds and identified 43 hit candidates (5.0%), 16 of them active on two or more substrates.
  • Training an activity prediction model on the Round 1 measurements (frozen ESM-C encoder with trainable pooling and projection heads); the ORFs with no detected activity serve as experimentally demonstrated negatives, so the selection criterion itself is updated each round.
  • Structural interpretation of hits with ESMFold and Foldseek fold-similarity search, plus FoldDisco analysis of the discontinuous catalytic motif (Ser–His–Asp triad, oxyanion hole).
  • Digitized the lab notebook and built a reagent, instrument and sample database so that experimental records are consistent enough to be reused as data.
