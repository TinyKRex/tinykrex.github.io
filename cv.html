Research Experience

Graduate Researcher — Synthetic Biology and Bioengineering Research Center, KRIBB

Feb 2025 – present, Daejeon · KAIST–KRIBB joint graduate program

Building an iterative Design–Build–Test–Learn workflow for enzyme discovery from metagenomes, in which protein language model embeddings select candidates and cell-free protein synthesis on a biofoundry measures what they do.

  • Narrowed ~5.3M ORFs from 16 environmental metagenomes to a tractable candidate set, recovering sequences too distant for homology search to return, by generating ProtT5 embeddings as a 1,024-dimensional vector database and ranking by cosine similarity to the mean embedding of 98 structurally characterized bacterial lipases.
  • Raised cell-free expression yield ~2.5× over a commercial kit by GFP comparison, and detected pNPO/pNPB/pNPP activity in the CalB control that the kit had not, by preparing S30 extract in-house from a GroEL/GroES-overexpressing strain; shortened library construction by carrying the T7 promoter/terminator in the primer 5′ tails, so that rounds run under identical conditions.
  • Identified 43 hit candidates (5.0%), 16 of them active on two or more substrates, by screening 855 candidate ORFs from 9 libraries against 3 pNP substrates on a 96/384-well automated workflow (Echo 525 acoustic dispenser, automated liquid handlers) and replacing ad-hoc thresholds with a robust Z-score criterion (Z ≥ 3 plus positive-control comparison).
  • Making the selection criterion itself update each round, by training an activity prediction model on the Round 1 measurements (frozen ESM-C encoder with trainable pooling and projection heads) in which the ORFs with no detected activity serve as experimentally demonstrated negatives rather than negatives guessed from sequence distance.
  • Structural interpretation of hits with ESMFold and Foldseek fold-similarity search, plus FoldDisco analysis of the discontinuous catalytic motif (Ser–His–Asp triad, oxyanion hole).
  • Digitized the lab notebook and built a reagent, instrument and sample database so that experimental records are consistent enough to be reused as data.
