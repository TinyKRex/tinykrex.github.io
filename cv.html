Research Experience

Graduate Researcher — Synthetic Biology and Bioengineering Research Center, KRIBB

Feb 2025 – present, Daejeon · KAIST–KRIBB joint graduate program

Building a closed Design–Build–Test–Learn platform for enzyme discovery from metagenomes, in which protein language model embeddings select candidates and cell-free protein synthesis on a biofoundry validates them.

  • Embedded ~6.9M ORFs from 8 environmental metagenomes into a 1,024-dimensional vector database; ranked by cosine similarity to the centroid of 97 curated lipases/esterases, recovering 976 candidates including remote homologs that alignment-based screening misses.
  • Redesigned the validation workflow: tailing PCR with T7 promoter/terminator in primer 5′ tails cut library construction from four steps to two; in-house S30 extract from a GroEL/GroES-overexpressing strain gave ~2.5× the yield of a commercial kit and detected pNPO/pNPB/pNPP activity the kit could not.
  • Standardized a 96/384-well expression–dilution–assay process on an Echo 525 acoustic dispenser and automated liquid handlers; screened 823 ORFs × 3 substrates for ~2,500 quantitative measurements. A robust Z-score criterion (MAD-based, Z ≥ 3 plus positive-control comparison) replaced ad-hoc thresholds, yielding 43 hit candidates (5.2%), 7 of them broad-specificity.
  • Training an activity predictor on Round 1 data (frozen ESM-C 600M encoder, ~6M trainable parameters); the 780 experimentally confirmed negatives define negative pairs for metric learning, so the selection criterion itself updates each round.
  • Structural interpretation with ESMFold at scale, Foldseek fold-similarity search, and FoldDisco analysis of discontinuous catalytic motifs (Ser–His–Asp triad, oxyanion hole).
