# I track down errors and cut out wasted steps.

*Bioengineering researcher with GMP quality assurance experience — looking for quality,
regulatory and clinical roles in pharma and biotech, or in AI-driven bio R&D and R&D planning.*

For **1 year 8 months in QA (Quality Systems)** at a biologics plant, my main job was supplier
management, alongside change control, deviation investigation and CAPA — finding the root cause of
what went wrong and carrying the fix through. At **FDA and overseas client audits** I served as the
English scribe and answered the supply-risk questions.

In **research** it is the same instinct pointed at the process. I cut a four-step library
construction down to two, replaced a commercial kit with in-house extract at ~2.5× the yield,
and threw out hand-set thresholds for a criterion that can be stated and repeated — which is
what made a screen of **5.3 million sequences → 855 tested → 43 that work** worth running at
all. The repetitive parts that were left, I moved into agents that run the same way every time.

M.S. candidate at the **KAIST Graduate School of Engineering Biology** (advisor: Prof. Haseong
Kim), student researcher at **KRIBB**. **Available from January 2027.**

## Highlights

- **GMP QA (Quality Systems), 1 yr 8 mo** at Hanmi Pharmaceutical's biologics plant: supplier
  management as the main role, plus change control, deviation investigation and CAPA.
- **English scribe at FDA and overseas client audits**, also answering the supply-risk questions.
- Recovered **43 active enzymes from 855 screened candidates**, 16 of them active on two or more
  substrates, by selecting candidates in embedding space instead of by sequence alignment and
  calling hits with a robust Z-score criterion.
- Searched **5.3 million metagenomic ORFs** for candidates by building a 1,024-dimensional
  ProtT5 embedding database and ranking by distance to a reference vector — a route that returns
  sequences too distant for homology search to find.
- Raised cell-free expression yield **~2.5×** over a commercial kit by preparing S30 extract
  in-house, and shortened library construction by carrying the T7 promoter and terminator in
  primer tails.
- Improved bacterial essential-gene prediction by up to **17%** over average pooling, as
  co-author on a **KMB 2025** poster on single-token protein embeddings.
- Cut the repetitive parts of my own research and job search out of the day by building
  **LLM agents** for paper translation and summary, meeting minutes, and a daily job-posting
  digest that runs itself on a schedule.
