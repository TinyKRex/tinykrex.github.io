# Publications & Presentations

## Conference presentations

**Global Representations in Overlooked Single-token Embeddings of Protein Language Models**
Seongbo Heo, **Yunkyeong Kang**, Gyeongmin Park, Seung-Goo Lee, Dae-Hee Lee, Haseong Kim
Poster Q-12, 52nd Annual Meeting of the Korean Society for Microbiology and Biotechnology
(KMB 2025), BEXCO, Busan, Korea — June 2025

> Protein language models are usually read out by averaging across residue tokens. We show that
> a single token's embedding can represent global information about the whole protein, and that
> using it for bacterial essential-gene prediction improves performance by up to 17% over
> average pooling.
